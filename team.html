The People

Meet the Team

The passionate locals behind Ballina Shire Youth Artists. Each person brings something unique to help our young artists shine.

Benita

Founder & Organiser

Benita is the driving force behind Ballina Shire Youth Artists. With a deep passion for music and a love for the local community, she saw a gap in performance opportunities for young people in the Ballina Shire and decided to do something about it.

From organising the very first Youth Mic night at Resonator Music to securing the Ballina Amphitheatre for a full band showcase, Benita has built this program from the ground up - connecting young artists with venues, mentors, and audiences who believe in them.

Her vision is simple: every young person deserves a stage. Whether they're a first-time performer or an experienced musician, Benita creates a safe, supportive space where creativity thrives and confidence grows.

Pete

Website Design

Pete is the creative behind the Ballina Shire Youth Artists website. A designer and developer based in the Northern Rivers, he volunteered his skills to help give the program a professional online presence that matches the energy of the events.

Inspired by Ela's hand-drawn poster artwork, Pete built the site with a pastel, hand-drawn aesthetic that captures the grassroots spirit of the program. He believes every community initiative deserves to look as good as it feels.
