Who We Are

A Stage for Every Young Voice

Reigniting local interest and involvement in the multi-faceted music industry, from the grassroots up.

Music. Community. Youth.

Ballina Shire Youth Artists is a not-for-profit program designed to reignite local interest and involvement in the multi-faceted music industry. We operate at a grassroots level, re-engaging many different aspects of the industry.

We boost the creative economy, education, wellbeing services, and local small business by giving young performers a platform to share their art with the community.

From open mic events to full band showcases, we create spaces where young musicians can practice, perform, network, and grow - building confidence and connections that last a lifetime.

What We Do

Building the Next Generation

A sustainable youth hub for music, art, and creative expression.

🎤

Youth Mic

Open mic events where young artists can perform in a supportive, all-ages environment. All contemporary genres welcome - rock, pop, hip-hop, indie, electronic, and everything in between.

🎸

Band Showcases

Bigger showcase events at local venues like the Ballina Amphitheatre, giving bands and solo artists a big stage experience in front of a real audience.

🤝

Mentorship

Connecting young artists with experienced local musicians and industry professionals. Intergenerational involvement brings a stronger, forward-thinking music scene.

🎥

Recording & Promo

Opportunities for youth to compile quick, easy promotional packages through live recording and visual content for their musical journey.

🌈

Networking

A hub where performers meet, jam, and form new musical groups. Building connections that turn into bands, collaborations, and lifelong friendships.

💛

Wellbeing

Mental health and wellbeing are strengthened through sharing music and art. Creative expression in a safe space builds confidence and community belonging.

Our Amazing Supporters

Venues & Sponsors

None of this would be possible without the incredible support of our local venues, sponsors, and community partners.

Featured Venue & Sponsor

Resonator Music & Tuition

Resonator Music has been the heartbeat of Ballina Shire Youth Artists from day one. More than a music store, they're a true community music hub - offering expert tuition, instrument repairs, rehearsal and recording studios, and equipment hire from their home at 9 Ascot Rd, Ballina.

They've provided the stage, professional sound, lighting, and instruments that make our Youth Mic events possible. Their passion for nurturing the next generation of musicians through service, learning, and community connection is what makes them extraordinary.

Sound Engineering

Founded by musician and producer Shea Roberts, Rockbox Recordings brings professional-grade sound engineering to our events. With roots spanning San Francisco and Australia, Shea's expertise in recording, production, and live sound ensures our young artists are heard at their absolute best.

headspace Ballina

Youth Mental Health & Wellbeing

headspace Ballina provides free support for young people aged 12-25 across mental health, wellbeing, education, and employment. With a “no wrong door” policy, they ensure every young person gets the help they need. Music and creative expression go hand-in-hand with mental wellbeing - and headspace is right there with us.

socialfutures.org.au →

MusicNSW

Peak Music Body

MusicNSW is the peak body for contemporary music in New South Wales - championing artists, communities, and music businesses from the ground up. Their programs support emerging musicians, regional touring, First Nations artists, and all-ages venues. They're building the future of music in NSW, and we're proud to be part of that vision.
